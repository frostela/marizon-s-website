# Marizon — Cinematography & Video Editing Portfolio

A live portfolio website built to showcase a cinematographer and video editor's creative work, visual storytelling, and color grading expertise.

🌐 **Live Website:** [https://www.marizon.in/](https://www.marizon.in/)

## 🎬 Overview

Marizon is a professional portfolio website developed for a cinematographer and video editor to showcase their work through an immersive and visually engaging digital experience.

The website emphasizes the quality of the artist's work, with a particular focus on cinematography, video editing, and color grading. Its design puts creative output first, allowing visitors to explore the portfolio and appreciate the attention to detail behind each project.

**Industry:** Cinematography / Video Editing

**Project Type:** Client Project · Professional Portfolio Website

**Status:** Live

## ✨ Key Features

- 🎥 **High-Quality Video Showcases** — Presenting creative work through immersive video displays.
- 🎨 **Interactive Color Grading Comparison** — A before-and-after slider that lets visitors compare ungraded footage with its color-graded version.
- 🎞️ **Immersive Visual Experience** — A design approach that keeps the artist's work at the center of attention.
- 🧭 **Clear Navigation** — Making it easy for visitors to explore the portfolio.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — Designed to provide a consistent experience across screen sizes.

## 🎨 Design & Development

The website was designed around a simple objective: present the artist's strongest work as clearly and impactfully as possible.

High-quality video showcases and an interactive comparison slider help communicate the technical precision and creative decisions involved in color grading. The overall experience prioritizes immersion, clarity, and visual storytelling while avoiding unnecessary distractions.

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Build a professional online presence for a cinematographer and video editor.
- Showcase creative projects through high-quality video presentations.
- Demonstrate color grading expertise with interactive comparisons.
- Create a memorable portfolio experience for prospective clients and collaborators.
